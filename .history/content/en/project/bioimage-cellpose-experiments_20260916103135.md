`Experimental`/`Prototype`

### Overview
Personal computational biology experiments evaluating deep learning-based cell segmentation tools (Cellpose) for the quantitative analysis of confocal and fluorescence microscopy images.

### Technical Scope
- **Python and Cellpose**: Evaluation of pre-trained cell segmentation models on confocal microscopy images.
- **Image Processing**: Batch feature extraction (cell area, signal intensity, cell count).
- **Jupyter Notebooks**: Exploratory pipelines for data analysis and visualization.

### Focus
Evaluating segmentation accuracy and automating cell counting workflows for bioimaging datasets.
